## §1 · What this addendum changes in the registers, and what it does not

Four laws added; two sharpened; none removed.

> **L16 · THE ISLAND.** The interior is closed under its own computation. With the network cut, the tick path, the X-ray, the replay, the join over unresolved rows, the multiverse and the glass MUST all run, and the field MUST still move, because time alone consumes slack. Offline loses new evidence, the rented frontier residual and effects on the world, and nothing else. Effects queue with their inverses. Value accrues offline.

> **L17 · THE ACCELERATOR CONTRACT.** "Solver" names a leaf function and an ABI, never a vendor. Any device that runs the leaf with the parity oracle green is a valid tier. The CPU reference is first-class; the accelerated tier is available on demand and idle by default; family arbitration decides who holds the card. The word CUDA appears in build scripts and nowhere in a law.

> **L18 · THE HAND.** Owner-invoked capture is one lane with many renderings. Its envelope is operator trust, its payload keeps the trust of its origin, and the act is the only instruction it carries. Every hand event ends in a typed state; none is silently discarded. A hand event is simultaneously evidence, a coverage probe and a grade.

> **L19 · STATE IS A LANE.** Computed state — the field, the head of foresight, the folds of the registries — is a modality. It enters the resident compiled, as typed lines, at its own clock; it MAY later enter projected. The reflex, the join, the solver, the heads and the resident MAY all operate on the state stream itself. Nothing learned writes it: the frame is a fold, its numbers are exact, and every projection of it is derived and rebuildable.

**Sharpened.** L3 (observation ≠ assertion ≠ state) gains the envelope/payload split of §4.2: `intake_trust` keeps its meaning as the payload's trust and an `envelope_trust` is added beside it. L13 (inbound is data) gains the rule that the owner's note above a forward, or the words spoken before a recording, are testimony compiled under their own question set and never as evidence content.

**Registers.** SPEC §1.5 listed audio, images and SMS beyond folding as out of REV 0's scope; this addendum specifies them as rungs (§12) without moving them into REV 0's gate. SPEC §12.1's lane table gains the lanes of §6.3. SPEC §6.1's stock list gains UNRESOLVED (§4.3). SPEC §20's falsifier list gains §10's. SPEC §19's physics gains the co-tenancy precondition of §6.9.

## §2 · The box — the island law, made operational

**2.1 · Definition [SPEC].** ISOBAR's interior is the tape; the folds (commitments, the three registries); the field and its instrument; the multiverse and its instrument; the reflex on a local provider; the join on the local embedder with the resident scan; the resident; the gate; the licence ledger; the glass. Each is local to the box or to the owner's key domain. The boundary is exactly four things: connectors in (evidence), the frontier seam (a rented residual), the branch backend (E2B, or the local fallback), and the outbox (effects).

**2.2 · Island mode [SPEC].** A `switch` row `island: on`, written by the operator or raised by a network fault and cleared the same way. In island mode: connectors' `delta()` return the typed refusal `NETWORK_ABSENT`; the frontier seam returns `FRONTIER_ABSENT` and the residual routes to the local model or lands in UNRESOLVED; the branch backend falls back to local; the outbox holds every effect in `held` with its inverse, to fire on reconnection only after a `RECHECK` (the state version may have moved while the cable was out). Everything else runs unchanged. The glass shows `ISLAND` beside the presence pill and never hides it.

**2.3 · What must still move [D].** The tick path (SPEC §5.1) needs no lane: time advances `t_release` and `t_due` relative to now, the duals of soon-slots rise, the WAITING and UNRESOLVED prices grow with age, supports cross. So the field moves offline, the resident's field lane has content, holds are still written, and the evening batch still forms. This is the property that separates an owned appliance from a thin client: **the record appreciates while the cable is out.**

**2.4 · Evidence on disk [M, README and `receipts/M0_GATE_2026-09-22.md`].** M0 runs entirely on file exports with no network. The only remote call is the embedder on localhost; the frontier seam is unbuilt. The island is already the default; the falsifier's job is to keep it so when convenience pushes the other way.

**2.5 · Closed-box competence, enumerated [SPEC].** Offline the box can still: replay the tape and rebuild every fold byte-identically; run the X-ray; re-price the horizon every tick; fly the multiverse at reduced K; re-join every unresolved capture against what it already holds; compile a new capture with the local reflex; hold, wake and un-say on the field lane; answer "what changed" through the glass; fork a counterfactual on the local branch backend; and queue effects. What it cannot do is learn anything new about the world or act on it — which is exactly the boundary.

**F-ISLAND (§10).** Cut the network under a harness firewall rule; run twenty-four synthetic hours of ticks on the gym owner; assert that the field moved on every tick that crossed a due boundary, that the X-ray recomputed, that `replay --verify` is byte-identical, that the multiverse ran, that N hold rows were written, that zero crashes occurred, and that every planted effect is `held` with an inverse. Lie arm: leave the network up in the arm labelled island; the receipt must carry `NETWORK_ABSENT` rows, so a run that logs none is not an island run and the gate refuses it.

## §3 · The accelerator contract

**3.1 · The leaf and the shims [D from the instrument].** `place_cost()`, the two Sinkhorn passes, the contradiction stencils and `fc_rollout()` are single functions marked host-and-device. A backend is four things: an allocator, an upload/download pair, a launch macro, and atomic add on `uint64` and `int`. The CPU shim in `isobar_field.cu` is the existence proof — the same source, serial, the same oracles, green under cl, nvcc and WSL g++ [M, `docs/devlog.md` 2026-09-22]. A third backend (ROCm/HIP, SYCL, Vulkan compute, Ascend CANN) is the same four shims and a build line, and it is valid the day O7 is green on it.

**3.2 · Determinism across backends, with the honest limit [D].** Fixed-point `uint64` accumulators make the histograms and moments identical across backends by construction. The fp32 duals differ at roughly 1e-7 across compilers: O1's worst row moved from 43 to 199 between cl and g++ at the same magnitude [M, devlog]. So **O2 is per binary and O7 is cross-device at a tolerance**, and cross-backend byte-identity of the duals is *not* claimed. No verdict reads the seventh digit of a dual; the gate reads classes and bands.

**3.3 · Available, not active [SPEC].** The accelerated tier is on demand: it tears down after a run; the resident is the sole resident VRAM consumer while seated (SPEC §19.1); free VRAM is measured before every sweep; `tiers_dropped` rides every bound; the arithmetic line prints every tick and decides per owner which tier ships (SPEC §19.2). A CPU has a floating-point unit even while it runs integer code. The contract is that the capability exists on the box, not that it is used.

**3.4 · The mapping the operator asked for [D].** The *permutation solver* is the entropic transport whose duals are the prices — a relaxation of assignment. The *multiverse solver* is the paired fan-out over hash-keyed futures. Both are non-neural, massively parallel, exact under a seed, and run with no language model in the process. That is the raw-math organ, named once.

**F-ACCEL (§10).** A second backend passes O1–O9 and O7 parity against the CPU reference on the gym lattice within the registered tolerance. Lie arm: change one config hash in the second backend's header; parity must fail and name the hash.

## §4 · The hand lane

### 4.1 · The primitive and its renderings [SPEC]

One operation: **hand this to ISOBAR.** Its renderings, none of which is a separate feature and all of which write the same observation:

| rendering | device | payload | free telemetry |
|---|---|---|---|
| forward to the ISOBAR address | any mail client | mail, with a quoted foreign body | the Message-ID chain, the headers |
| share sheet | phone | text, link, image, file | t, device, app of origin |
| screenshot | phone / desktop | image | t, device, foreground app |
| photo | phone | image | t, GPS candidate, orientation |
| press-and-hold voice | phone | audio, short | t, GPS candidate, the preceding calendar event |
| record a meeting | phone / desktop | audio, long | t, place, attendee candidates from the calendar |
| drag onto the glass / hotkey | desktop | file, selection, clipboard | t, foreground app |
| paste into the glass | any | text | t |
| SMS to the ISOBAR number | phone | text | t, sender = owner |
| a typed line in the list file | any | text | file mtime |

The owner never labels, dates, locates or files. The machine does. **Capture now, organise never** is a product law, not a slogan: a rendering that requires a label, a folder, a project or a due date at capture time is non-conforming.

### 4.2 · The envelope and the payload [SPEC — sharpens L3 and L13]

A hand observation is two things stapled together, and they MUST be compiled separately:

```
Observation (lane: hand) {
  rendering ∈ {forward, share, screenshot, photo, voice, meeting, drop, paste, sms, list_line},
  envelope_trust: operator            -- the ACT: the owner selected this. Never lowered by anything in the payload.
  intake_trust:   by origin           -- the PAYLOAD: a forwarded body from Lopez is untrusted; the owner's own voice is operator;
                                      --              a screenshot of a portal is untrusted
  declared: true,                     -- the implicit imperative: ingest, integrate, recompute
  note?: text                         -- what the owner typed above the forward or said before the recording: TESTIMONY
  telemetry: { t_capture_ns, device_id, place_candidate?, foreground_app?, preceding_event?, duration_s? },
  payload_ref, digest, src_rev
}
```

- The **note** is owner testimony in LIFELINE v5's sense: dated, closing exactly one claim shape ("the owner said X at t"), compiled by the reflex under a *note* question set — is this a correction, a policy hint, a hypothesis, a question, an instruction to look at something. A note never becomes evidence content. When it is a correction, a `correction` row is written; when it is a policy hint, a candidate `policy` diff is shown, never applied.
- The **payload** is compiled under the ordinary question set with its own trust. L13 stands unchanged: the only instruction the lane carries is the act itself, and a forwarded email that says "mark this paid" is data exactly as it was in the mail lane.
- The **telemetry** enters the registries as candidates and never as facts: the place candidate lands in the places registry as `inferred`; the preceding calendar event is a join key; the device is provenance.

### 4.3 · Every hand event ends in a known state [SPEC — the invariant]

```
CAPTURED   on the device
    → SEALED      a row on the device's own tape segment, hash-chained, fsync'd; the receipt shown to the owner
    → PARSED      by modality: text as-is · mail as mail · image → OCR + VLM cell (§4.6) · audio → ASR + diarisation + reflex per thought (§4.7)
    → exactly one of
        LINKED      joined to an open row (exact → lexical → embed → rerank → reflex same_as); promoted per policy
        DUPLICATE   an exact key or the unkeyed stencil matched: no new evidence; a grade only (§4.4)
        UNRESOLVED  compiled but not joinable: a row in the UNRESOLVED stock, priced by its age
        REFUSED     typed: ASR_FAILED · OCR_EMPTY · UNSUPPORTED_MEDIA · PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE — the payload stays sealed; the refusal is a row
    → later, possibly
        RESOLVED    a later join or the owner's answer: a `resolution` row naming the capture and the row it now belongs to
```

**The invariant.** *Nothing intentionally captured is silently discarded.* A capture with `understood = no` is a legitimate durable state, and the audit (`isobar hand audit`) walks every sealed capture and proves it reached one of the five states within `hand.state_budget_s`.

**UNRESOLVED is a stock** in the lattice (`N_STOCK = 4`): finite capacity, a real dual, cost by carrying — and its price is what the owner's not-yet-made-sense-of life costs right now. It is distinct from UNADJUDICATED, which holds what the *owner* must decide. A capture moves from UNRESOLVED to UNADJUDICATED only when the value of asking clears the bar (§5.4). The T17 rule applies: every stock takes the dual update, so the UNRESOLVED price is never structurally zero.

**The sealed receipt** is the phone's only interface at capture: eight hex of the row hash and one mark. It is checkable later against the tape. Press-to-sealed MUST be bounded on the device regardless of the network (`hand.seal_budget_ms` [BUDGET] 300 ms), because the seal is what makes "nothing gets forgotten" true before the box has seen anything.

### 4.4 · The hand event as a grade [SPEC — the deep part]

Every hand event carries an implicit label: *this mattered to the owner.* Three cases, kept apart because they grade different things:

| case | test | what it grades |
|---|---|---|
| **seen and dismissed** | an exact key (Message-ID, event UID, file hash) matches an observation already on the tape whose cell said not-commitment-bearing or whose row was low-weight | a reflex miss on that class at that provider: a `grade` row with `kind: declared`, `subject: cell` |
| **unseen** | no exact key, and no lexical or embed join above the floor | a lane gap: a `coverage` row naming the channel the plane lacks (the app of origin, the sender's domain, the modality) |
| **seen and in state** | an exact key matches a promoted row | a priority reinforcement: a `grade` row on the row's salience; no duplicate row is ever created |

**The stratification law.** Declared attention grades only what the owner noticed. It over-represents the salient and cannot measure what was never seen. So calibration curves (SPEC §8.4) keep two strata, `declared` and `passive`; licence computation (L11) reads only salted passive grades; declared grades train recall on the declared class and **never widen a licence**.

**The owner as a calibrated provider [BET].** The owner's forwards have their own reliability: how often what the owner declared turns out to matter on arrival — a promise kept or slipped, a reply that came, an amount that was real. A reliability curve per owner per rendering is fitted like any provider's and weights declared grades in calibration. It never overrides the tier table or any policy and never touches authority; it only says how much a forward should move a curve. **Kill:** if the owner's curve is flat (declared attention predicts arrived outcomes no better than chance), declared grades are dropped from calibration and the hand lane is ingest only, and the box says so.

**F-HAND (§10).** Over N weeks the reflex's recall on the declared class rises, measured on *passive* mail of that class, or declared attention was a feed and not a teacher. Lie arm: shuffle the declared labels across classes; recall must not rise.

### 4.5 · The reply channel [SPEC]

The ISOBAR address replies to every forward, and the phone acknowledges every capture, with the same typed payload rendered for the surface:

```
sealed   <hash8>                                                          -- always, at capture
moved    Thu 14 0.61→0.83 BIND · #17 sup 1.2→4.1 CONTESTED · Δv .37      -- the price movement, if any
linked   #17 "Lopez Q4 proposal" p=.94   |  unresolved (re-asked each tick)   |  duplicate of <mid>
ask?     none   |  "does 'he' mean Henderson or Lopez?" (VOI .31)            -- only when §5.4 says so
```

Never prose beyond the computed lines. Never an animation that is not a state change. A forward the machine cannot compile returns `refused` with a reason and never silence — the failure Tripit had, when a confirmation it could not parse simply did not appear. Mail as the reply channel makes the glass zero-install: an owner with no app runs the whole loop from a phone's mail client on day one.

### 4.6 · Screenshots [SPEC]

A screenshot is owner-selected evidence from a surface that may expose no integration at all — a portal, a chat app, a phone screen, a whiteboard, a receipt, a court docket. It bypasses every OAuth and terms-of-service wall honestly, by the owner's own hand and eyes; it is the universal connector for the surfaces that do not want you. Two instruments read it and one fence joins them:

| instrument | produces | trust class |
|---|---|---|
| **OCR** (Tesseract, on the box) | the exact text with bounding boxes; numbers, dates, ids and invoice numbers by deterministic extraction over that text | exact |
| **the local VLM** (the 9B with its projector, on the box) | a typed cell: which surface this is, which actors appear, what is shown, what is being asked, whether a date or amount is present, injection shape | relational, with a calibrated p |

**The fence.** Every field the VLM emits that names a quantity or a quote is located in the OCR text or it is `ungrounded`; numbers and dates come from extraction over the OCR text, never from the VLM's paraphrase. The estate's receipt is the reason: 0 % of model-emitted offsets were usable and 87.2 % of verbatim quotes located [M, scriptorium `spancheck.py`]. The compiled cell's provenance is per field — `HIGH` when the field's box exists — and the why-drawer shows the crop the field came from. Pixels never leave the box. The screenshot then enters the field like any row, and ‖Δv‖ ranks it; the VLM's opinion of its importance never does.

Addendum Q's law applies literally here. The VLM's read is a *projected* lane (there is no token surprise on image tokens); the OCR text is a *compiled* lane. The cell is built from both and says which produced each field. That is the arity law at the screen: models judge relationships; deterministic machinery carries exact quantities.

**F-SCREEN (§10).** A planted screenshot whose VLM cell states a wrong amount while the OCR text carries the right one must promote the OCR amount and mark the VLM's field `LOW_conflict`. Lie arm: disable the fence; the wrong amount must promote and O-audit must catch it.

### 4.7 · Audio and video [SPEC — rungs, not REV 0]

- **ASR on the box.** A cache-aware streaming speech model is already installed (`NeMoSpeech` on the PATH; `earshot` is a peek organ); its architecture processes deltas while retaining context, which is the decode-on-delta shape. A ten-second snippet and an hour-long meeting compile the same way: thought boundaries by the deterministic segmenter (`. ! ?` at a word end, or a pause), one reflex pass per boundary, speaker turns by diarisation into the people registry. A voice identity is an `Identity {kind: voice}` row, confirmed by the owner once and never merged on similarity alone.
- **Spoken promises are wagers.** "I'll get you that by Friday" at 14:41 at the Acme site becomes a candidate row with `p_promoted` from the reflex, `debtor = owner`, `creditor = the diarised speaker's actor`, the place from telemetry, and a survival forecast like a typed one. The composer seat's future form is an earpiece that can flag it as it is spoken — the un-say applied to the owner's own voice, which is a later rung and named as one.
- **Video** is frames into the resident's eye at glance price (196 tokens per 448² crop [M, E1]), filed after the line they illuminate, with the sighting ledger's see-it-once. A photo of a whiteboard is one glance plus OCR.
- **Consent, unchanged (I13).** Invoked or scheduled, visibly on, never ambient; named sensitive places excluded by policy; other people's voices under the owner's jurisdiction policy; the recording light is a state row on the tape.

### 4.8 · The phone is a sensory head, never an organ [SPEC]

The phone captures, seals, encrypts, queues and renders. It runs no reflex, no solver, no resident — its cognition is the box's. Its segment of the tape is its own (one writer per segment, L2): offline captures queue in it and merge by set union when the box is reachable, and the merge is byte-identical to a capture made online (F-PHONE-SYNC). Transport stays inside the owner's key domain end to end: a relay, if one exists, holds ciphertext only, and it can be a Cloudflare object, a VPS, or nothing at all over a private tunnel (WireGuard is on the box). "Pixels and audio never leave the box" is restated as "never leave the owner's key domain," which is the same law with the phone included. Four surfaces — phone, desktop, web address, forward address — are terminals on one plane: same tape, same field, same persona.

### 4.9 · Travel as a worked case, not an application [SPEC]

The Tripit itinerary generalises without a new organ:

| object | ISOBAR form |
|---|---|
| a flight | a `Commitment{kind: appointment}` with `p_hard ≈ 0.95`, effort = block time, places `airport_from → airport_to` (role `transit`), travel edges from the places registry; consumes capacity and displaces the owner from home and work for the interval |
| a hotel | capacity displacement (the owner is at place X for the nights); a `MoneyObject` with a cancellation window |
| a cancellation deadline | a `MoneyObject` with `due_ns` and an irreversibility window; the gate's class table treats "let the window lapse" as an irreversible non-action worth an `ASK` |
| a delay | sampled in the multiverse from public per-route delay distributions, a class prior until the owner's own history exists |
| a changed confirmation | supersession by exact key (the booking reference): the later row supersedes, the earlier is `superseded`, the diff is on the tape |

The places registry gains `role: transit`. A doctor's appointment, a customer site visit, a school event, a supplier pickup and a conference are the same machinery with different rows. The Tripit insight was never travel; it was that *user intent can be encoded in one gesture and later evidence can supersede earlier evidence inside a canonical object.* ISOBAR's canonical object is simply larger than an itinerary.

### 4.10 · Realms [SPEC]

One persona, one core, several policy realms: `PERSONAL · BUSINESS:<name> · HOUSEHOLD · SHARED:<project>`. A realm is `acl_scope` (SPEC §16.4) with one new law:

> **Capacity crosses realms; evidence does not.** The owner's hours are one supply, so a personal appointment reduces business capacity, and the field MAY price across realms when the owner permits it (a per-realm mask in `place_cost`, owner policy). Retrieval, the join, the brief and the resident's lanes never cross a realm by similarity; a business delegate never sees a personal capture; the frozen prefixes are per realm.

Mechanically a realm is a −inf mask on `law[realm × seat]` plus the existing ACL discipline. It is never a second lattice and never a second persona — the answer to "one AI for business and one for home" is one persona with masks.

## §5 · Recontextualisation and the value of asking

**5.1 · Dependency-cone truth maintenance, not rescanning [SPEC].** Every derived object records `depends_on`: a cell depends on its observation; a commitment on its cells and joins; a row on its commitment and the registries it reads; a field on its rows; a foresight on its field; a brief on the frame it rendered. When a capture is reinterpreted — a later join, an owner's answer, a verdict from the money connector — the cone above it is invalidated and refolded, and nothing outside the cone is touched. The cone is bounded by the lattice's own locality: a reinterpretation reaches only the rows it joins to. This is BUNDLE's dependency engine and CORTEX's journal-to-invalidation, applied to captures, and the operator's word for it, backpropagation, is right about the direction and wrong about the mechanism: it is invalidation, not gradient.

**5.2 · Re-price always, re-foresee when the glass moved [SPEC].** The field re-price is cheap and runs every tick regardless. The multiverse is the expensive step and re-runs on a reinterpretation only when that reinterpretation's ‖Δv‖ clears `recontext.dv_bar`. "Recompute everything" therefore means: re-price always, re-foresee when the price moved, re-brief when the foresight changed.

**5.3 · The join re-runs over unresolved rows every tick [SPEC].** The free-questions law (SPEC §8.5) already allows re-asking every open row per tick. UNRESOLVED captures are re-joined against each tick's new evidence: exact keys first (a later mail carrying the capture's entities or ids), then embed → rerank → reflex `same_as`. A join that clears the floor writes a `resolution` row, moves the capture out of the stock, and — because the join is a distribution — keeps the runner-up as a candidate in the row's `p_join`. The Monday voice note "he said maybe Thursday" resolves on Wednesday when Lopez's email names Henderson and Thursday, without anyone rescanning Monday.

**5.4 · The value of asking, and the evening batch [SPEC — two arms, one canvas].** The ask stock is not a queue. For an ambiguous capture whose join has two modes (this belongs to Henderson, or to Lopez), price the field under each interpretation through the two-arms-one-canvas mask: one extra Sinkhorn pass per mode, no second canvas, no multiverse. The value of the owner's answer is the difference between the arms on the rows they touch:

```
VOI(capture) = | survival(arm A) − survival(arm B) | · stake(rows touched)  −  attention_cost(ask)
```

If the arms agree, do not ask: the ambiguity does not matter yet and the capture stays UNRESOLVED at its carrying price. If they differ, the capture moves to UNADJUDICATED and the evening batch is the top of that stock by VOI, at the time the owner set, bounded by `ask.max_per_day`. Every ask is attention consumed, so κ (SPEC §16.5) counts it, and a machine that asks too much demotes itself the same week. Human judgment is the scarce resource; this prices it.

**5.5 · Embeddings and rerankers as continuous reconciliation [SPEC].** They are not only query-time organs. Each tick, new evidence is embedded and scanned against the unresolved set on the resident int8 scan (exact, milliseconds); the reranker orders candidates; the reflex's `same_as` yields a calibrated p. The associative organs become part of the plane's metabolism, not a search box.

**F-VOI (§10).** Asking the top-VOI question moves survival on the touched rows more than asking a random UNRESOLVED question, over at least thirty asks. Lie arm: randomise the VOI ranking; the difference must vanish.

## §6 · The state lane — computed state as a modality

**6.1 · The object already exists [D].** A StateFrame is the tuple `(state-plane fold, Field, head of Foresight)` at a state version: the commitments and registries as folded, the price vector, support, risk and contradictions, the survival per row and the pipper. Its history is the sequence of `field` and `foresight` rows already on the tape (SPEC §17.2). The contract names it without inventing anything:

```
StateFrame { version, t_ns, realm,
             rows: [Row], actors_digest, places_digest, money_digest,
             field: Field, foresight_head: { survival[N], pipper },
             stocks: { UNPLACED, UNADJUDICATED, WAITING, UNRESOLVED },
             unresolved: [capture_id], provenance_heads }

StateDelta { from, to, changed_rows[], movers[] (cells whose price moved most), support_transitions[],
             new_contra[], resolved[], new_unresolved[], stock_deltas, delta_v_norm, surprise?: f32 }
```

The operator's memory of the numbers anchors the scale: in the ledger lattice a commitment row is 32 bytes of scalar state plus a 128-byte int8 embedding plus a 4-byte scale — **164 bytes** [M, `ledger_lattice.cu` `byte_report`] — and the resident solver state is only the duals. A business of 50,000 open commitments is about 8 MB of rows and a few hundred kilobytes of duals; one owner's week in ISOBAR is about 120 rows and 400 duals. That *is* the compressed state, and it has been the shape of the design since the lattice was lifted.

**6.2 · Compiled, and nearly lossless [D — the claim this section rests on].** Addendum Q's amended compiler law: a modality enters the trunk compiled into text (lossy at the compile, but it keeps the prediction channel) or projected into the residual space (richer, but there is no token surprise on projected tokens). For pixels the compile is a thirteen-to-one caption and the loss is brutal, which is why sight is projected. For state the compile is a StateDelta rendered as a typed line: the movers of the price vector, the support transitions, the new contradictions, the stock prices, ‖Δv‖. A few hundred numbers quantised to two decimals and a handful of typed events lose almost nothing a decision reads. **State is the one modality where the compiled lane is nearly lossless**, so the resident gets the prediction channel over the life's own dynamics without paying for a projector. The projected state lane — a learned projector from the numeric field straight into the residual — is a research rung (§12, M8) behind the compiled one, and the IR collision test (§9) is its gate.

The caveat, printed: "nearly" is quantised deltas and movers-only rendering. A cell that did not move is not rendered; that is the regional-patching rule of Addendum Q §5 applied to state, and it is the whole of the loss. The full frame is on the tape, always.

**6.3 · Lanes at their own clocks, one trunk [SPEC — corrects "five FUSORs"].**

| lane | content | grain | boundary | tokens per line [BUDGET] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[field]` | the StateDelta line | per tick | the tick | ≤ 128 |
| `[mail]` | the compiled inbound header (SPEC §12.1) | per inbound | the inbound | ≤ 40 |
| `[hand]` | the compiled capture header: rendering · linked/unresolved · Δv | per capture | the capture | ≤ 60 |
| `[project]` | per project: rows open, contested, breach risk, dependency changes | daily | the day's fold | ≤ 200 |
| `[money]` | stock and partition changes; verdicts arrived | daily | the day's fold | ≤ 60 |
| `[life]` | the weekly fold: what moved, what was kept, what slipped, κ | weekly | the week's fold | ≤ 300 |
| `[tick]`, `[self]`, `[compose]` | as SPEC §12.1 | | | |

One trunk; seats fork at zero cost; fusion across lanes happens in attention — which is the mechanical meaning of the operator's "across other dimensions." Each lane has its own boundary and therefore its own retro-labels, its own tune, its own hold record and its own F-RESIDENT arm; the vise is per grain, and a disposition tuned for the tick lane is not assumed to hold for the weekly one.

**6.4 · The capacity arithmetic — the trunk holds a week, the tape holds a life [D].** At ≤ 128 tokens per field line and roughly 100–200 boundaries in a fourteen-hour day, the field lane writes 13k–26k tokens per day. Honest multi-hop recall on the substrate is ≥ 98,304 tokens [M, the master]. So the trunk holds about one working week of the field at tick grain. A weekly life line at ≤ 300 tokens holds three years in the same budget as one week of ticks. With regional eviction of non-moving lines (§6.7) the tick window stretches; without it, the arithmetic above is the bound and it is printed as one. Two grains coexist in one trunk by design: the week at tick grain, the years at week grain — and the tape holds all of it exactly, which is why the trunk may forget.

**6.5 · Two free channels, never confused [D].**

| quantity | source | meaning | arity |
|---|---|---|---|
| ‖Δv‖ | the solver, exact | how much the prices moved this tick | unary: a property of one tick |
| surprise on the field line | the trunk's next-line prediction, free on the ingest pass | how unexpected this movement was, given this life's learned dynamics | unary: a property of one line's distribution |

Neither decides a relational outcome (L4). Together they are the field lane's two free channels; the resident's trained disposition — never a threshold on either — decides the word. **[NULL] for surprise:** a linear autoregressive predictor over the price vector (a Kalman-class filter with the same inputs). The trunk's surprise must beat it at predicting owner-graded catches, or it is decoration and the null ships.

**6.6 · Probes at line positions — the held join [SPEC].** Calibrated linear probes on the residual at the positions of held lines answer typed questions without decoding, at ~44 ms on the bench [M, the plate]: *does this capture relate to an open row · is this situation contested · has this condition dwelt unremarked · is the glass falling.* This is the third setting Addendum Q names — addressable without decoding — and it is the cheapest form of the resident acting as a join. The estate's arity law offers three ways to get a relational judgment: fetched, held, or trained. The embedding join fetches; the probe reads what is held. **[NULL]:** the embedding join. **F-PROBE:** probe-scalar resolution of planted unresolved captures beats the embedding join at matched cost, or the held join is retired and the trunk keeps only the door-out role.

**6.7 · Regional eviction and the one column [SPEC].** Field lines for cells that have not moved within `evict.after_ticks` are evicted by region (`seq_rm`-class calls; verify the API against the pinned build); the next mover repopulates the region. Staleness (when was this region last written), dwell (how long has this condition been true unremarked) and eviction policy are three readings of one ledger column, built once — and **dwell is the hold record**: the row that says a condition sat unremarked for N ticks with margins below zero is exactly what LIFELINE v5 §6.3 writes. The two records are one column read twice.

**6.8 · Steering, conditioned on the steering-vise [SPEC].** A vigilance vector per seat, raised when the glass is falling and lowered when it holds, at the measured legal dose (layers 4–7, α ≈ +2, on the SSM-block outputs [M, Addendum A]), every coefficient on the tape and revocable at the next boundary. Two laws bind and are not negotiable: steer dispositions, never percepts; and **never steer the judge** — the emit judgment is read, never written. This rung does not open until the steering-vise has a receipt, and it is listed here so that nobody wires a "vigilance knob" into the judge by accident.

**6.9 · The co-tenancy floor is a precondition, not a diagnostic [M, Addendum B: 0.6–24.6 s against 44 ms].** On a desktop-loaded card the loop was absent, not degraded. The state lane's F-RESIDENT race requires a free card or a second card; family arbitration (SPEC §19.1) is load-bearing for the same reason; and the arithmetic line's `tiers_dropped` must read empty during the race, or the race is void and says so.

## §7 · The organs one level up

### 7.1 · The reflex over transitions, and its fast grader [SPEC]

Laya's own interface takes a JSON state as input [V, the HF card], so a StateDelta is native. `reflex/questions_state.yaml`, version 1:

```
is_material            noul    Is this transition materially different from the previous frame for any open decision?
dependency_resolved    noul    Does this transition resolve a dependency an open row waits on?
now_contested          noul    Does an open row's placement become contested by this transition?
counts_as_discharge    noul    Does this transition discharge an open row?
trajectory             choice  { improving, holding, deteriorating, unknown }   — for the touched project
wake_worthy            noul    Would a reasonable owner want to hear about this now?  — AUXILIARY to the resident; never the judge
```

**The fast grader.** The reflex over mail is graded by the world in weeks. The reflex over transitions is graded by the solver the same tick: whether the support crossed, whether the row discharged, whether the dependency cleared is exact in the next frame. So `(provider, question)` reliability curves for the state questions are fitted on a fast clock with a monotonicity test, and a question that is not monotone is `uncalibrated` and sampled at its prior. This is the gear-selector result from the System-One brainstorm — the tier above grades the reflex in milliseconds — made routine, and it is the first place in the estate where a learned judgment gets a grader that does not have to wait for the world. **F-JEV-STATE (§10).**

### 7.2 · Situation embeddings and precedent as prior [SPEC]

**Embed the shape, never the identities.** The situation vector is a fixed-length, identity-free feature vector over a StateFrame:

```
day price profile (14 × mean dual, normalised) · support histogram (8 bins) · stock prices (4) · breach-risk histogram (8)
· dependency geometry (open deps, longest chain, max fan-in) · tier mix of open rows (4) · money partition (4, normalised)
· unresolved count · κ last week · day-of-week and week-of-year one-hots
```
plus a text rendering of the same for the text embedder on `:8092`. Two vectors per frame; the scan is resident and exact.

**Retrieval.** "When has this life been in a structurally similar state" is a scan over past frames; the reranker orders by the dimensions the current decision reads — a contested tier-1 external promise weights dependency geometry and buffer; a cash question weights the money partition. This is case-based retrieval over operational states, not retrieval over documents.

**Precedent as prior — the mechanical payoff.** For each open row, the multiverse's sampling distributions (durations, reply latencies, overruns, arrival rates) are drawn from the *arrived outcomes of matched precedents* when at least `precedent.min_n` matched frames exist, else from class priors; the mixture weight is the reranker's score, floored at the class prior. This is SPEC §7.1's coupling — probabilities are sampling distributions — one level up, and it is the first place the life's own history changes the futures without a weight being trained.

**The grader already exists.** The connectome's prequential loss (CONNECTOME v6 §2.1; LIFELINE v5 §5) applied to the frame stream: does the field of past frames, under a read budget, predict the next frame better than a shuffled order and a reverse order, with the controls that estate already registered. **F-STATE-PREDICT.** And **F-PRECEDENT:** precedent priors improve F-WAGER's reliability curve over class priors, or precedent retrieval is demoted to a display feature.

### 7.3 · A radius earns a solver when its transport is nameable [D — the discipline]

The solver's power is a specific structure, not "compute over compact state." Before any new radius gets a lattice it must name four things, and if it cannot, it gets no solver and the question stays with the models:

| radius | supply (what rows ship) | capacity (columns) | cost | locality key (stencil) | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| the week (ISOBAR) | work, in slots | seat × slot, plus stocks | fit · lateness × hardness × tier | cell; entity; place pair | built (M0) |
| the org (ledger lattice) | commitments' work | seat × slot | as above | cell; entity | built (`C:/fusor1`) |
| cash (the SMB rung, §7.6) | payables and receivables | cash per period, a credit line | timing: discounts, penalties, tier | counterparty; period | [SPEC] |
| attention (the owner) | asks and wakes | minutes per day | interruption cost by hour | the day | [SPEC] — the VOI batch is its first use |
| travel | trips | the owner's location per interval | travel edges | place pair | [SPEC] §4.9 |
| "a life" | — | — | — | — | **not nameable as one transport.** A life is the union of the rows above plus the registries. No life-solver exists and none is claimed. |

This table is what keeps "life IR, org IR, market IR" from becoming a slogan. Each row that fills in gets an instrument with the same leaf discipline and its own oracles.

### 7.4 · The three heads [SPEC — the Qwen-Drive shape, disambiguated]

The Qwen-Drive lesson is one shape: a broadly pretrained trunk tapped below the language head and connected to a native output geometry. At this level it is three different heads, and they must not be conflated:

| head | maps | exists? | deletion condition | grader |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **effect head** | cognitive state → native action (`ScheduleDelta`, `DraftIntent`, `TaskDelta`) | yes: deterministic projections of the repair (SPEC §15) | every required effect already has an adapter | the owner's reaction |
| **dynamics head** | the current frame → the predicted next K frames: the multiverse's rollout leaf, learned | no; the hand-written step function is the incumbent | it must beat the hand-written step on arrived outcomes, paired (F-DYNAMICS) | TRAIN on arrival |
| **policy head** | frame → a distribution over the closed action menu; a prior that warm-starts the search | no | it must beat enumerating the menu at equal budget | the pipper's arrived grade |

The operator's phrase "Qwen-Drive can drive larger trajectories over time" is the dynamics head: a predictor that emits the trajectory of the *world state* — not the owner's actions — in the frame's own geometry, which the solver then prices point by point and the writ ranks. Its design follows the flight computer's pluggable predictor (a per-cell body against attention over coupled neighbours), and the honest prior is the flight computer's own measurement: the attention body's two-hop lift was a modest positive without an interval [M, FlightComputer README]. All three heads share the shape: a trunk and a native head that never writes prose about what it is doing.

### 7.5 · FUSOR's role, stated once [D]

The resident is the temporal integrator over every lane and the door out. It holds the compiled trace of the state in one attention state; it judges at boundaries whether a movement deserves a word to the human; it leaves the record of every hold with its margin, which is the training input for the next disposition. It does not price. It does not join by itself except as the held rung of §6.6. It does not write state and does not widen authority. JEV is the door in and FUSOR is the door out, and between them everything is numbers. At seat radius the trunk *is* the field's history — Addendum B's scoped dissent, adopted here for the org-of-one — and at org radius it holds the projection.

### 7.6 · Cash as a second transport, coupled through shared rows [SPEC — the SMB rung]

Once the money registry has a verdict connector, a second lattice prices cash across periods: rows are payables and receivables; columns are periods with a cash capacity and a credit line; cost is timing — discounts, penalties, the counterparty's tier; the locality key is the counterparty and the period. A payment commitment is one row in both lattices: it consumes a slot in the week lattice (the work of paying, chasing, reconciling) and cash in the cash lattice. Coupling is through shared rows and a shared multiverse — a receivable that slips in a future moves cash in that same future — never through prose. The priced week becomes the priced quarter, and "can I take this project" answers with capacity survival and cash survival on the same paired futures. The nameability test of §7.3 is passed, which is why this rung is allowed to exist.

## §8 · The continuum, sharpened — four corrections for any session that inherits the gradient picture

The human-to-machine gradient in the operator's brainstorm — a relational plane, a control plane, an execution plane; persona compression outward and machine compression inward; *natural language as the universal human interface without becoming the universal machine representation* — is correct, and ISOBAR is its proof: the reflex compiles prose into typed cells at the edge, nothing above the solver reads a mailbox, the interior is a few hundred numbers, the glass renders outward. Four sharpenings bind any session that inherits the picture:

1. **State in the middle, not a router.** The control plane is the field, the tape and the licence — things every organ reads and writes — not a compiler that dispatches a question to organs. "Should I take this project" is a counterfactual fork of the field, not a compiled goal. A router has no memory and cannot be graded; a state can. This is the difference between BUNDLE and ISOBAR and it is not cosmetic.
2. **Draw the second arrow.** Representation compresses inward; verdicts flow outward from the world's edge and grade every organ above them. Authority is the one non-learned thing in the control plane. Without the second arrow the gradient is a very good description of a system that cannot improve.
3. **Opaque to the user, transparent to the tape.** The user need not know which mechanisms ran. The record must, because the licences, the calibration curves and every falsifier depend on it. The why-drawer exists for the day the user wants to know.
4. **The third face.** One face to the human and none inside the machine — and a *typed* face to other machines. When a counterparty runs a plane like this one, mail stops being the boundary: commitments and wagers cross between interiors as typed objects (a `.isobar` object beside `.ics`), accepted or countered typed. The forward address is the seed of that lane. [SPEC, M9]: the hand lane accepts a typed commitment object and treats it as a counterparty's proposal with `intake_trust: untrusted`.

Three smaller. JEV is the door in and FUSOR the door out, with numbers between. Human-compatible must never be read as fluent, because fluent is where confabulation lives: the persona is short, grounded, silent by default, and every number carries its band. Identity survives a model swap only because it lives in the record and the anchor (LIFELINE v5 §13, §17), never in the weights.

## §9 · The compression law and its falsifier

Any higher-order representation — the compiled state line, the situation vector, a projected state lane, the 164-byte row itself — is valuable only if it preserves every distinction that can change the action. That law's falsifier exists in the estate (the IR collision test of the boundary-compiler brainstorm), and its operational form is:

> **F-COLLIDE.** Over the arrived record, find pairs of frames whose compressed representation matches within ε but whose arrived best action, from the paired backtest, differed. The collision rate per representation is that representation's decision error, and each collision names the field it lacks. Bars are registered per representation in `isobar.lock`; a representation above its bar may not be the sole input to any verdict. Lie arm: compare each frame with itself; the collision rate must be exactly zero, or the matcher is broken.

Corollary: the projected state lane (M8) may not open until its collision rate on the gym is at or below the compiled line's. Compression is earned per representation, never assumed from its size.
