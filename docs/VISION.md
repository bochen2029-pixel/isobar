# VISION — where ISOBAR is going

*Non-normative. `docs/SPEC.md` governs what is built; this page records the ambition the next spec revisions will draw on, so a reader of the repository can see the shape of the whole before the parts exist. Written 2026-09-22.*

## The box

Imagine a box of computation you own. Inside it: a resident mind, a typed reflex, an embedder, a solver on whatever accelerator is present, a local model, a tape. Cut it off from the world and it still computes on everything it holds: it re-prices your horizon, flies your futures, replays its record, re-joins what it knows. What it loses offline is new evidence, the rented frontier residual, and effects on the world. What it keeps is your state. That is the difference between a cognitive appliance you own and a thin client whose value is zero the day the API is down.

Two things are non-negotiable inside the box. A frontier language model reachable through one seam, on-prem when local models suffice and rented when they do not. And massively parallel non-neural computation — a permutation solver, a multiverse solver — as a permanent organ, on demand even when idle, on any accelerator that runs the shared leaf with parity. Not because the card is glamorous. Because once semantics have been compiled into a compact typed state, raw parallel arithmetic over that state is the only thing that can price contention across a thousand promises or integrate over ten thousand futures before you act.

## The hand

"I need to note that down" fails for one reason: the cost of a note is not the writing. It is the labeling, dating, locating, filing, and later finding. The phone already knows when, where, and can see and hear, and it is always in the pocket. So the primitive is one act — forward, share, photograph, speak, record, drop, hotkey — with zero labels. The clock, the place and the modality are free provenance. The only thing to remember is *hand it to the machine*.

Everything handed becomes durable evidence on the tape. Everything the machine can resolve becomes structured state. Everything currently consequential becomes the live field. Everything worth deciding is rehearsed computationally before either the machine or the human has to act. What cannot be resolved sits in a stock with a price, and the machine asks about it only when the difference between its two readings changes what you should do.

## The continuum

Toward the human the system becomes maximally human-compatible: one persistent persona, short, grounded, silent by default, that knows the individual and survives the model beneath it changing. Toward the work it becomes maximally machine-native: typed state, prices, distributions, graphs, solver variables, with almost no language inside. Natural language is the universal human interface without being the universal machine representation. Persona compression outward; machine compression inward. One face to the human; no faces inside the machine.

In the middle sits state, not a router: the field, the tape, and the licence. Representation compresses inward; verdicts flow outward. Outcomes arrive at the world's edge and grade every organ above them, and authority is the one thing in the middle that is never learned.

## State as a modality

The field's own frames — a few hundred numbers and a handful of typed transitions per tick — are the one modality that compiles into the resident's lane almost losslessly. So the resident can hold the trajectory of a life or a business in one attention state, notice a movement that its own model of you did not expect, and answer typed questions about what it holds with probes at the line positions, never by decoding. The reflex becomes a predicate unit over transitions, graded by the solver the same tick. The embedder recalls the shape of a situation, never its identities, so precedent becomes the multiverse's prior. The solver rehearses trajectories through state space. A learned head projects intent straight into the surface's action geometry. Every one of these operates one level above words, and every one is graded by what arrives.

A radius earns a solver only when its transport is nameable: what is conserved, what bounds it, what it costs, and where contention is local. A compressed state earns its size only when it preserves every distinction that can change the action, and the collision test is the audit.

## The record is the moat

The cognition is the demo. Every organ above gets cheaper and better every quarter without anyone's help. What appreciates is the graded record: the price history of every hour, the wager history with outcomes, the record of every hold with the margin it held by, the reliability curves of every question the reflex has ever answered, your own corrections. A better model makes every one of those more valuable and none of them free.
